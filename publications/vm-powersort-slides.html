1 · powersort

Powersort merges runs in nearly optimal order

Runs. One scan finds the already sorted runs.
Powers. The interval between two run midpoints drops onto a tree; the pole it hits is the boundary’s power.
Stack. A smaller power triggers merges. Cost ≤ n(H + 2); in CPython since 3.11.

the catch

Every Timsort / Powersort implementation merges through a buffer of n/2 elements. Merging in place exists — but is several times slower.
