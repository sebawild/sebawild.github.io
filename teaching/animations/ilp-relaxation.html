Integer programs and LP relaxations

Finding a solution and checking it is easy. Proving that it cannot be beaten is the hard part: it is a statement about all solutions. Choose a vertex cover (or an independent set), try to prove that it is optimal by brute force, then let the LP relaxation do it with a single number.

An integer program asks for the best grid point of a region; its LP relaxation allows every point of the region. Push the line (in 3D the plane) of equal objective value in the better direction: it stops at the best grid point first, and leaves the region only later, at the LP optimum. The band in between is the gap. Click a grid point to take it as your solution.
