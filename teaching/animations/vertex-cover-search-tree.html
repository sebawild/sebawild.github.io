Vertex Cover  ·  the bounded search tree, step by step

Every edge needs one of its endpoints in the cover, so we can branch on which one, and every branch spends budget: the parameter $k$ drops in each recursive call, and the search stops at $k = 0$. The tree on the right is that search; its size depends only on $k$, not on the size of the graph. simpleFptVertexCover branches on an edge (branching vector $(1,1)$, at most $2^k$ leaves); betterFptVertexCover branches on a vertex $v$ of maximal degree versus all of its neighbours ($(1,d)$ with $d \ge 3$) and solves graphs of maximal degree 2 directly. Click a node of the tree to jump there.

Input
The algs4 graph format, as written by the graph editor: the number of vertices V, the number of edges E, then E lines v w (vertices are 0…V−1; anything after the two endpoints, such as a weight, is ignored). Optionally V more lines x y give the drawing: any scale, y pointing down, the picture keeps its aspect ratio. Without them the graph is laid out automatically.

A line k 5 (or k = 5) anywhere sets the parameter; lines starting with # are comments. Parallel edges are merged, self-loops are refused.

The tree is computed in full before it is shown, so very large instances are refused: at most 60 vertices and 4000 recursive calls.
