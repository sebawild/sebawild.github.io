How to read it · input format · keys

The manual on the left is the algorithm. Each numbered plate is one instruction of the poster, and the plate that is highlighted is the instruction being carried out right now. Click a plate to jump to its next occurrence.

Top drawing is the close-up of the current instruction — a shelf being broken in two, or the balance-and-conveyor merging machine. Bottom drawing is the whole tree of recursive calls at once: every shelf that Merge Sört ever creates, drawn in its place. Bars travel down the tree when a shelf is split, and back up when two sorted halves are merged. A star marks a shelf that is finished.

Input format: whitespace- or comma-separated tokens, as in the algs4 sorting test files — e.g. S O R T E X A M P L E or 24 8 15 42 4 16 23. Numeric input is compared and drawn by value, so repeated values give equal-height bars and a balanced scale; anything else is compared as text and drawn by rank. Up to 20 items.

Keys: → / ← step, space play or pause, Home / End jump to the ends, d presentation mode, Esc leave it.

Presentation mode hides everything except the two drawings and the current plate, and starts playing; the same keys still work, and clicking anywhere pauses or resumes.
