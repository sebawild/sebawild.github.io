Input as text
The elements, separated by spaces, commas or line breaks — the format of the algs4 sorting clients, so e.g. tiny.txt can be pasted as it is. If every element is a number they are compared numerically, otherwise as strings (String.compareTo). Lines starting with # are comments.

Optional settings on lines of their own, handy for prepared examples: @algorithm quick (one of selection bubble bubbleImproved insertion binaryInsertion merge mergeCheck mergeBU powersort quick), @pivot first (random, first, m3), @seed 7 for reproducible random pivots, and for powersort @runs inc (both, inc) and @power def (fast, def).

At most 64 elements. The same settings also work in the page address, e.g. #steps&algo=quick&pivot=first&in=6,4,7,1,3,8,5,10,2,9.
