About Me

I am a Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at University of Marburg, where I’m heading the Algorithms Group. I also hold a part-time post as Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at University of Liverpool, where I am part of the Algorithms and Complexity and Networks research groups, as well as the TCS @ Liverpool cluster.

On this site, I collect information about my work on algorithms, records about courses I taught and various comments on computer science, math, technology, typography, language, and whatever else I found worth sharing or preserving.

Previously, I held a postdoc position in Ian Munro’s group at University of Waterloo and worked as researcher and instructor in the CS department at TU Kaiserslautern (now RPTU), where I did my PhD in the group of Markus Nebel.
