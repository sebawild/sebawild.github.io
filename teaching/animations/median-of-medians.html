wild-inter.net Selection by rank

Median of Medians

Deterministic selection by rank, in place. The pivot is not guessed: the input is cut into groups of five, each group is sorted, the five medians are gathered at the front of the range, and the algorithm calls itself to select the median of those — that pivot is then good enough to throw away a constant fraction every round. Everything happens inside the one array; the frame shows the range still in play.

Two black boxes

      
sort and partition are not unfolded here — only their effect on the array is
        animated. Their cost is charged as stated: sort of a block counts the comparisons an insertion sort makes
        on it, partition counts r − l.

      
Note what partition works on: the whole range, including the up to four leftover
        elements at the end that were never part of any group of five. Only the medians of complete groups take part in
        choosing the pivot.
